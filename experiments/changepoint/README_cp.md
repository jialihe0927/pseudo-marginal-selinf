# Changepoint experiments

The changepoint application is organized into four focused experiments:

- [Sampling efficiency comparison](efficiency_comparison/README_efficiency_cp.md): organized and reproducible from the supplied result object.
- [Global-null calibration](inference_null/README_inference_null_cp.md): organized code, frozen paired p-values, and the paper ECDF figure.
- [Non-null inference and power](inference_nonnull/README_inference_nonnull_cp.md): Full-selector, Full-path and Data Fission directional tests, paired frozen results and the paper figure.
- [Sampling accuracy validation](sampling_validation/README_sampling_validation_cp.md): full saved samples, offline diagnostics and the paper coordinate ECDFs.

Sampling efficiency, global-null calibration, directional power and sampling validation
are included as runnable public code.
